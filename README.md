### Tips for Best Results

#### Photo Capture Guide
- **Overlap:** Make sure each photo shares about 60% overlap with the previous one
- **Stability:** Keep the camera level and move in a smooth arc around your subject
- **Lighting:** Use consistent lighting—avoid moving from sunlight to shadow
- **Focus:** Keep your subject sharp and in focus for every shot

#### Scene Selection
VGGT-Diff works best with:
- ✅ Static scenes (no moving objects or people)
- ✅ Textured surfaces (helps the AI understand depth)
- ✅ Well-lit environments
- ✅ Subjects with clear boundaries

Try to avoid:
- ❌ Highly reflective or transparent objects
- ❌ Completely flat, blank walls
- ❌ Rapidly changing lighting conditions
- ❌ Extremely thin or delicate structures
